# Original spy-heist score

Two original instrumental cues generated on the user's active paid ElevenLabs Starter account on 2026-10-04. No movie soundtrack was downloaded, sampled or uploaded as a reference. Prompts request genre-level espionage traits and explicitly exclude existing melodies. `*.request.json` contains the exact prompts; receipts contain the provider request/song identifiers where supplied, never credentials. Original MP3 masters remain in the private creative production archive.

- Vault Infiltration: restrained staccato strings, plucked bass, muted trumpet and ticking percussion; a propulsive asymmetric spy groove.
- Midnight Pursuit: faster low strings, snare/toms and brass accents for action.

The game alternates these two tracks by level number, including campaign missions 13–100 and beyond. The previous five-track playlist is no longer imported into the app.

Each 60-second source is normalized toward -20 LUFS / -2 dBTP, then its final second crossfades into the first second. Body plus seam creates a 59-second loop. AAC 128 kbps, 44.1 kHz. Music fades with the existing player and ducks during alarms (0.22 multiplier vs 0.34 normal) to leave space for effects. Pausing or leaving gameplay stops playback.
