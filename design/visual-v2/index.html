Asset production

How the next assets should be made.

01 / MATCH

One camera and one palette.

Use the existing seeker sheets for identity, materials and proportions. Keep the overhead angle fixed. Large forms must read at phone size.

02 / SEPARATE

Keep each layer reusable.

Floor, cover, actor, shadow and effects stay separate. The level data positions objects. Art should never invent an obstacle or a safe route.

03 / VERIFY

Check it in motion.

Inspect alpha and frame anchors, then test on the dark floor. Check controls, guard visibility, dash, cosmetic variants and reduced effects.
