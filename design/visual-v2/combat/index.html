TARGET HEALTH + DIFFERENT BEHAVIOUR

More than the same robot with extra HP.

Proposed starting balance. Courier: 100 HP, 25 damage per shot, 0.4s fire interval. No paid stat boosts.

Scout

50 HP

2 shots to disable.

Fast patrol. One shot after a clear aim warning. Duck behind cover.

Sentry

75 HP

3 shots to disable.

Holds a lane. Two-shot burst followed by a recovery window. Move between bursts.

Heavy

150 HP

6 shots to disable.

Slow movement. Wide shot pattern and a longer wind-up. Take the flank.

Warden

200 HP

8 shots to disable.

Final elite. Two familiar attack patterns. No unexplained invulnerability.

Firing is a decision

Tap a guard to select it. The courier approaches if needed, stops and shoots. Tap the floor to cancel and move. It never fires at unselected enemies or shoots through cover.

Stealing changes the room

Pickup triggers the alarm, faster pursuit and marked reserve doors. Escape with the phone; you do not need to clear every optional guard.

Fair losses, fast retries

Aim warnings, visible shots and cover give the player a chance to react. No instant death from a filled detection bar. The tutorial rewinds failed steps.

Keep the weekly challenge fair

Three missions, five ranked starts each, free practice. Same combat stats. Score extraction, time and remaining health—not spending or kill farming.

Twelve different heists.
