What changed

Rankings open first. Server ranks are preserved, including ties. Your position and play button stay below the scrollable results. “Near you” shows real nearby entries. Empty and offline states never invent a rival or prize.

Battery dash and sharper phones

Dash is not active in the current tap-to-move combat. The old battery field and mission ID remain internally. Adding a dash would need a separate balance pass.

Android currently shows 18 rendered views at 384 × 472 per view. The phone screen texture is only 156 × 333 pixels. Better source textures, a detailed master model and studio reflections should come first. Keep Android’s safe turntable, then increase image detail using individual frames with a small cache. Do not load a giant high-resolution atlas for every phone.
