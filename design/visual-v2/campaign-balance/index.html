What makes escape harder

  • Courier speed drops from 4.1 to 3.15 after picking up the phone.

  • Sentries aim for only 0.6 seconds. Cover blocks bullets, but guards keep searching.

Method, limits and what “end to end” means here

Every trial uses the shipping simulation and is independently checked by the server replay verifier, locally. The 12 wins also pass through mission unlock, completion, serialization and reload in the progress model. This does not certify browser gestures, payment, account sync or Android persistence.

The reference solver can look ahead by simulating outcomes. The basic controllers cannot; they also do not intelligently dodge bursts. Perturbed plans keep following their command sequence rather than replanning. A failed controller does not prove a mission is impossible.

All spawns survive eight seconds of idle input with full health. All reference clears meet the time-star target. Tutorial simulation passes all eight steps with full health. No live attempts, scores or payments were submitted.
