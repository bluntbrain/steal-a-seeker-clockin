The new flow

All 12 free. No wallet. First clear: 100 credits, plus 25 for each extra star. Retry freely.

Practice free. One pass unlocks three weekly missions, five ranked chances each. No paid extra chances.

What informed the changes

  • Hunter Assassin's developer listing: cover, stealth, progression and character rewards. No claim to know its internal tuning.
  • Apple's game onboarding guidance: teach one action at a time and let people play before buying.
  • Android's game-loop guidance: consistent timing and responsive rendering.
  • Game-feel research survey: coordinated input and audiovisual feedback.

Checks and next gates
