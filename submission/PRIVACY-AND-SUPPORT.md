# Privacy and support — pre-release draft

Steal a Seeker stores preferences, tutorial status, mission progress and pending submission recovery on your device. Optional playtest recording is off by default. When enabled, it keeps at most 100 attempts with game events and recent performance measurements. The app does not automatically upload that report. You can export or delete it in Settings; deletion also stops recording.

When you connect a wallet and use the online devnet service, it processes your public wallet address, signed login challenge, purchase/transaction references, owned items, saved campaign progress and submitted input replay. It uses these to verify access, reconcile test-token payments and compute game results. Public leaderboard entries display shortened wallet addresses and scores. Solana transactions, including test-network transactions, are public records; deleting a local app save cannot delete them.

The app never asks for a seed phrase or private wallet key. Wallet approval happens in your selected wallet. Server-operated collection/return signing keys are not part of the app. TEST SKR has no monetary value. Mainnet payments are not available in this review release.

Before public service launch, the owner must publish the actual support contact, hosting/RPC providers, server retention/deletion policy and jurisdictional privacy notice. They are not finalized in this draft. Do not publish this file as a complete store privacy policy until those operational details are filled in and reviewed.

For the private playtest, send feedback directly to the builder who supplied the APK. Include app version, Android model, a description and optional exported report. Do not send seed phrases, private keys or payment credentials.
