# Level 11 — Silent Circuit

The target is the Archive phone in the top-right corner. The exit is back near your starting point, at bottom left. Two horizontal barriers divide the room. Each has a relay door that opens for nine seconds, with a switch on both sides.

1. From the start, go up the left side and approach the lower relay pad, below the left-hand door. Release the joystick and press **ACT**. The pad only works while you are stationary. Cross up through the opening before its countdown ends.
2. In the middle room, cross toward the relay below the upper-right door. Watch the guard's amber cone. Use cover and wait for it to face away before crossing its route. If needed, face an open lane away from your intended route and throw a decoy, then move while the guard investigates.
3. Stop at the right relay, press **ACT**, and go up through that door. The phone is near the top-right corner. Stop and hold **TAKE** to collect it.
4. The alarm accelerates guards. On your way back down, use the relay **above** the right-hand door to reopen it. Cross down into the middle room.
5. Head left through the middle room and use the relay **above** the left-hand door. Cross down, then return along the left side to the bottom-left **EXIT**. Stay inside for one second to finish.

## If you are stuck

- **ACT does nothing:** release the joystick, let the courier stop, then press ACT. You must be within roughly one tile of the pad. Release the action button before pressing again.
- **The door closed:** return to the switch on your current side and activate it again. You do not need to reach the original switch from the start.
- **Guard sees you:** break sight behind a crate first. A guard currently watching you ignores the decoy. Throw the decoy away from the crossing you want to use.
- **Don't rush the entire mission:** the target time is 3:30, but the hard limit is 7:00. Only each relay crossing has the short nine-second window.
- **Dash is optional:** it works while carrying and costs 20 charge. It is not required to open relays.

This guide is also available in **Pause → Settings** while playing level 11. The UI shows relay time while a door is powered. There are four relay activations on the normal out-and-back route; timing around guards can vary.
