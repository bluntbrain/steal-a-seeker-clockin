I

First, how the game actually works today

The pitch in your note assumes the first twelve levels are free and the Game Pass unlocks the rest. That is not what the app does. Every level is free, from 1 to 1000. The Game Pass is a one time bundle: 3,500 credits and the Ghost Signal outfit, priced at 500 SKR (about 10 dollars) with a planned regular price of 1000 SKR (20 dollars). The paywall itself says "The whole campaign is free. Skip to play." The rebate (SKR back after twelve heists) is a server setting that is currently off, so no player sees it.

Sources

Paywall copy and prices from the live catalog as shown in the web preview on 4 October 2026; pass bundle in shared/commerce.ts; level gating in src/campaign/levels.ts (a level is locked only by progress and engine version, never by the pass); rebate setting CAMPAIGN_REBATE_SKR on Railway, read 5 October 2026.

Two things to clear before any prize post goes out

Prizes. A cash prize for a leaderboard is a contest. When the entry is a paid Game Pass, the question of whether it counts as a paid entry game of skill, and what Indian and other laws say about that, is open. Name it to counsel; do not resolve it in a tweet. Until then, every post marked "after counsel" stays in draft.

Likeness. The seven bosses carry the names and faces of real people. Posting a boss card that tags that person is a public use of their likeness. Ask each of them first, keep the reply, and only then post. Every boss post is marked "consent first".

Claims. "The game pays for your Seeker" is an earnings claim. The calendar words it as a comparison of two numbers (pass price, weekly prize) and says nobody has done it yet. Keep it that way, and never state player counts or install numbers without a dated source.

II

The growth loop in one picture

The app already has a share card for a campaign completion and level specific sharing. Before 12 October the card needs the level number large, the stars, the time, a boss badge when a boss fell, the handle @StealASeeker and an install link. That is the one asset the whole plan depends on.

III

Four weeks, four themes

  • Week 1, 5 to 11 October: the build and the submission. CLOCK IN closes on 8 October. Posts show the guards, the maze generator, the thousand levels, and the Seeker specific feel. Tag @SolanaMobile, @RadiantsDAO and @SuperteamIN on the submission posts only.
  • Week 2, 12 to 18 October: share cards and bosses. The share card ships, then one boss reveal every second day with the trait line as the hook. Each boss post tags the person it is based on, after consent.
  • Week 3, 19 to 25 October: how the board works, rewards announced. Explain total points and server verification. Announce weekly rewards only if counsel has cleared them and the weekly board has shipped.
  • Week 4, 26 October to 1 November: the first reward week. Live week, a verified gameplay video, two skits, the winner post with the transaction.
  • 2 to 5 November: month one recap and month two roadmap. Boss presentation pass, weekly rewards every Monday, community rooms.

Posting rules

  • Personal account at 20:00 IST (14:30 UTC, morning on the US east coast). Official account at 21:30 IST. Same day, different wording, never the same text twice.
  • Tag a person only in the post that is about them. Tag @SolanaMobile on Seeker specific posts, not daily. Never tag more than three accounts in one post.
  • Every post carries one asset. No text only posts except the winner announcement, which carries the transaction link.
  • No numbers about players, installs or revenue until there is a dated source. Posts marked "no numbers" are written to stay true without them.
  • Helius and Phantom handles were not verified to a high confidence on 5 October, so they are not tagged anywhere.
